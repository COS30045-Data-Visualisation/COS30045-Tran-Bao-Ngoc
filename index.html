Appliance energy in Australian homes

Small loads add up

Heating, cooling and hot water usually use the most energy in a home. Televisions, computers and kitchen appliances use less each, but they run every day and the total is easy to overlook.

Read the energy label

Many appliances sold in Australia carry an Energy Rating Label that shows a star rating and an estimated number of kilowatt hours per year. It is the quickest way to compare two models.

Standby is still use

An appliance that is switched off at the remote can still draw a small amount of power. Over a whole year, even a small standby draw becomes a measurable number on the bill.
